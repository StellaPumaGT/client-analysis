Altria Group, Inc.
Altria Group is the largest U.S. tobacco company by domestic market share, operating through wholly-owned subsidiaries Philip Morris USA (Marlboro, Basic), John Middleton (Black & Mild cigars), USSTC (Copenhagen, Skoal), and Helix Innovations (on! nicotine pouches). The company is executing a “Moving Beyond Smoking®” transformation strategy while defending Marlboro’s 59.6% premium cigarette share and investing in smoke-free oral nicotine products. As of Q2 2026, adjusted EPS grew 4.9% H1 and the company returned ~$3.9B to shareholders in six months.
